## 🎓 Teaching the System Prompt

### Key Takeaways:
1. **The system prompt guides responses** - Every response follows the guidelines
2. **On-topic questions get enthusiastic, helpful answers** - Full product info
3. **Off-topic questions get polite redirects** - Still friendly, not dismissive
4. **Mixed questions focus on the beauty aspect** - Ignores unrelated details
5. **Tone is always warm and encouraging** - Makes users feel valued

### For Students:
- Notice how the AI ALWAYS stays focused on L'Oréal/beauty
- See how polite refusals redirect rather than shut down
- Understand that even "no" can be helpful and positive
- Learn how AI instruction affects output quality
